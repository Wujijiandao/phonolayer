# Official Learning Guides — v0.9.2

The public sample corpus now includes five original conceptual learning guides. They are deliberately short enough to inspect inside the editor and rich enough to exercise mixed-language annotation profiles.

The guides formalize these project principles:

1. **语言材料分开，元语言知识连起来。** Keep target-language material linguistically identified; connect concepts and learning strategies at a higher layer.
2. **文、形、声相关但不等同。** Context/document, orthographic form and phonological knowledge are separate data relations.
3. **真实读音优先于机械拆字。** Whole-word or lexical readings take precedence over artificial character-by-character alignment.
4. **先回忆，再揭示。** Retrieval behavior is recorded separately from direct reveal; behavioral evidence is not automatically converted into a mastery claim.
5. **迁移是线索，不是证明。** Similarity across languages supports comparison but does not establish identical sound systems, morphology, origin or meaning.
6. **Personal Memory 不是词典。** It represents the learner's confirmed history and uncertainty states rather than an external answer authority.

These are design and learning-workflow principles. The public package does not claim that PhonoLayer has already demonstrated superior learning outcomes in a controlled experiment.
