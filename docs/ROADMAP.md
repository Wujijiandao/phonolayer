# Public Roadmap

## 0.9.x public beta

- external Windows testing and bug fixing;
- migration and corruption recovery hardening;
- onboarding and documentation;
- accessibility and i18n cleanup;
- performance on large documents;
- validate whether real users understand and continue using the Manual-first workflow.

## Learning-state research

Reading/pitch, recognition/production, hesitation/failure, and retrieval-event time series remain research targets. PhonoLayer will not silently turn these signals into an automatic mastery score until the semantics and evidence are strong enough.

## Possible future service

Optional official encrypted sync/backup may be explored only after users demonstrate a real need. It is not required for the local desktop core.

## v1.0

A v1.0 compatibility promise requires stable file/database migration, reliable Windows distribution, robust recovery, and sufficient external beta evidence.
